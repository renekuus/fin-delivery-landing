2. Our role: intermediary, not restaurant

Fin Delivery operates a platform on which restaurants offer their products and customers order them. Three consequences follow:

  • The contract for the food is between you and the restaurant. The restaurant is the seller of the food. It is responsible for the contents, quality, food safety, allergen information, and for the product matching its description.
  • The contract for the delivery service is between you and Fin Delivery. We are responsible for the order being collected and delivered to you properly.
  • We collect payment on the restaurant's behalf. Paying us discharges your payment obligation to the restaurant.

We do not prepare or store food. We do not determine restaurants' menus or recipes.

4. Ordering and formation of the contract

Products and prices shown in the app are an invitation to make an offer. Your order is the offer, and the contract is formed when the restaurant accepts it and you receive confirmation in the app.

A restaurant may decline an order — for example because an item has run out, because of a rush, or because of opening hours. In that case no contract is formed, nothing is charged to you, and any charge already made is refunded.

Check the contents of your order before confirming. Information concerning special diets or allergies should be verified directly with the restaurant — we cannot guarantee that cross-contamination is avoided in a restaurant's kitchen.

6. Cancellation

6.1 Why there is no 14-day withdrawal right

Consumers generally have a 14-day right of withdrawal in distance selling. It does not apply to prepared food. Article 16 of the Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU) excludes the right of withdrawal for, among other things, goods liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly, and for catering services supplied on a specific date or within a specific period.

This is not a term we impose; it is the law, and it applies to every food delivery service.

6.2 When you can cancel

You can cancel free of charge up until the restaurant has begun preparing your order. Cancel in the app or by contacting us. Once preparation has started the order can no longer be cancelled, because the food has already been made for you.

If you cancel before preparation begins, we refund the full amount.

6.3 If we or the restaurant cancel

If an order cannot be delivered — the restaurant closes, an item runs out, no courier is available — we tell you and refund the payment in full.

7. Delivery

The delivery time shown in the app is an estimate, not a binding promise. Traffic, weather and restaurant load affect it.

Make sure you are reachable at the address you gave at the time of delivery and that your phone is available. The courier will contact you if needed.

If you cannot be reached, the courier waits a reasonable time and tries to contact you. If no contact is made, the order is deemed delivered and is not refunded — food does not keep while waiting. If you have instructed us to leave the order at the door, the order is deemed delivered once it has been left in accordance with your instruction.

The customer is responsible for a failed delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete address.
